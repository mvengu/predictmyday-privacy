PredictMyDay – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026.

Developer: the PredictMyDay developer Contact: mvengu@gmail.com

Summary

PredictMyDay keeps your information on your phone. The app has no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics. It does not request any Android permission and does not use the internet connection to send anything anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage:

  • Birth profile you enter: date of birth, time of birth (or that it is unknown), the name, coordinates and time zone of your birthplace, and optionally the Moon sign you choose when your birth time is unknown. It is used only to work out your Moon sign and birth star.
  • Daily outlooks you open, with the calculation details (scores, the rules that applied, planet positions, version numbers, and your Moon sign and birth star). The record does not contain your birth date, time or coordinates.
  • Your own ratings and notes (1 to 5 stars and an optional note up to 200 characters) for a day, if you choose to add them.
  • Preferences: language, colour theme, light/dark mode, and whether you finished the welcome screens.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, advertising ID or location. The birthplace is chosen from a list inside the app or typed by you; the app never reads your device location.
  • It does not send data to the developer or to any third party, and it contains no advertising or analytics code.
  • It does not include your birth details in anything it shares. The Share button passes only the date, the overall score and its label to the Android share sheet; you choose where it goes.
  • Data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied off your phone by those features.

Place data

The built-in place list is based on GeoNames (geonames.org, CC BY 4.0). It is searched on your phone; no search text leaves the device.

Your choices and deleting your data

In Settings you can edit your birth profile, clear the saved history (this also removes ratings and notes), or delete the birth profile (this also clears the history). You can remove a single day’s rating on that day’s screen. Uninstalling the app removes all of the stored data.

Children

PredictMyDay is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Not advice

The scores are traditional astrology interpretations for personal reflection. They are not scientifically validated probabilities and are not medical, financial, legal or other professional advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date, and the app’s Settings will describe any change in what is stored.
